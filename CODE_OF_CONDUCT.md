# Code of conduct

Help make bagaev a useful place to question ideas and improve software.
Treat participants with respect across experience levels and backgrounds.
Critique claims and behavior; offer concrete evidence and leave room to revise
an opinion.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, and
publication of another person's private information are not welcome. Do not
use project spaces for spam or pressure campaigns. These expectations apply
to Issues, Discussions, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close a discussion, or restrict
participation in proportion to the conduct. They should explain moderation
without exposing private reports or personal information.

To flag public conduct without repeating harmful details, ask a maintainer in
the relevant thread. If a report needs privacy, request a confidential channel
without naming affected people or posting details; share them only after the
channel is verified. Use GitHub's platform reporting tools for abuse requiring
platform action. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
